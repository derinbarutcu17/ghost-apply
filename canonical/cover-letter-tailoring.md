# Cover-letter tailoring

Cover letters must be specific, evidence-grounded, and concise. Use the live
role brief, the candidate claim ledger, and projects actually present in the
private portfolio allowlist. Prior letters are style references, never evidence.

## Structure

1. Start with a real observation or motivation connected to the company's user,
   product, or working problem.
2. Connect two or three verified candidate strengths to the role.
3. Prove one claim with a real project or experience; do not list a portfolio
   project that is absent from the allowlist.
4. Close with a direct, low-pressure statement about adding value.

Avoid generic praise, unsupported metrics, invented customer stories, inflated
seniority, and keyword stuffing. Use the candidate's natural voice. An AI tool
may help explore wording, but the final copy must be checked against the claim
ledger and the company's actual work.
